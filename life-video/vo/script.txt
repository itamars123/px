In the beginning... there was only silence.
And then... light.
From dust and fire, a world was born.
Life began small. Impossibly small.
And slowly... it learned to grow.
A first heartbeat.
Childhood. Everything is new. Everything is possible.
Youth. To run. To dream. To dare.
Then someone arrives... and the whole world changes.
There are storms. There are falls.
But we always rise again.
Time moves on. The seasons turn.
And what remains... is the light we leave in others.
Because every ending... is a new beginning.
This is life. Live it. All the way.
