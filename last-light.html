One night at a time

Each round is a night. The horde gets bigger, faster and tougher, about 13% more health every night you survive.

01 · Dusk

The lights go out

The dark closes in over the first seconds of each night. Your flashlight is the only thing that shows what's coming.

02 · Night

Hold the street

Night 1 sends 14 of them. Every night after adds 6 more. Kill everything and pick up the scrap they drop.

03 · Dawn

Spend the scrap

Patch up, buy armor and better rounds, or save for the shotgun and SMG. Then the sun sets again.
